NOVA OS

Privacy

Last updated November 6, 2026

Nova OS is a free game desktop. We collect as little as we can, there are no ads, and we never sell anything.

Playing as a guest

Your scores, settings, saves and XP stay in your browser. Nothing is sent to us except that you're online (see below).

With an account

  • We store your email address, the username you pick, and your game data (scores, XP, achievements, settings, saves) so it syncs between devices. It's stored with our database provider, Supabase.
  • Sign in with Google: Google shares your name, email address and profile picture so we can create your account. We only use them to sign you in. Your Google name is never shown — you pick a username.

What other people can see

  • Your username, avatar color and scores/XP on leaderboards.
  • Game likes and requests are counted anonymously.
  • The site owner can see who is online right now and which game they're playing, and can send fun on-screen effects (like confetti).

Your choices

You can play without an account. To delete your account and its data, email us from the address on your account.

Games

Hosted games are open-source projects made by other people (see About & Credits in Nova). They run on our site and don't send data anywhere else.
